# Privacy Policy — Digest

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

Digest ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application that summarizes content you share using AI. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide the service.

### Account information
- **Apple ID email** — only if you choose to share it during Sign in with Apple
- **Display name** — optional, from Sign in with Apple
- **User ID** — an anonymous identifier we generate to link your saved items to your account

### Content you save
- **URLs, text, images, and files you share to Digest** — processed and stored so we can show you the summaries
- **AI-generated summaries** — the output of our AI processing

### Technical data
- **Push notification token** — only if you allow notifications
- **Anonymous crash reports** — diagnostic data to improve the app
- **Subscription status** — whether you have a free or pro subscription
- **IP address** — temporarily processed by our servers and infrastructure providers when you make requests; not stored long-term

### What we do NOT collect
- We do not collect your location
- We do not collect your contacts
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not sell your data
- We do not use your data for advertising or profiling

## Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the legal basis of **contract performance (GDPR Article 6(1)(b))** — this data is necessary to deliver the service you signed up for. Without it we cannot run the app for you. We do not rely on consent for advertising or profiling because we do neither.

## How we use your data

- Process shared content with AI and return summaries to you
- Deliver push notifications when a summary is ready
- Verify your subscription status
- Diagnose and fix crashes (anonymous only)

## Third parties that process your data

To provide the service, we rely on the following processors. Each receives only the minimum data required to perform its function.

| Processor | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Gemini API)** | Generate AI summaries | The content you share (URL contents, text, images, files) |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple, Push Notification delivery, payment processing | Apple ID identifier, push token, purchase receipts |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription verification and management | User ID, subscription status, purchase events |
| **Cloudflare (R2 storage + CDN)** | Store thumbnails, deliver assets | Generated thumbnail images, request IP |
| **Hetzner (via Coolify)** | Application hosting | All request data passes through this host |
| **Jina AI (Reader API)** | Read web pages that block direct access | The address (URL) of the page you saved |
| **Webshare** | Proxy network used to fetch YouTube and Reddit pages | The address (URL) being fetched, no account data |
| **Sentry** | Anonymous crash and error reporting | Crash stack traces, device model, OS version (no content) |
| **PostHog** | Anonymous product analytics | Aggregated event counts, no content |

We may change processors over time to improve the service; we will update this policy if the categories of processing change materially.

## International data transfers

We are based in Türkiye. The processors listed above operate servers in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and the United States. When your data is transferred outside your country of residence, it is protected by the legal terms of the receiving processor (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards where required by GDPR).

## Content rights

- Summaries are generated from content you share. Content remains owned by its original creators.
- Digest stores the source URL and summary for your personal use only.

## Data retention

- **Account data (email, display name, user ID)** — kept until you delete your account
- **Saved items and summaries** — kept until you delete them or delete your account
- **Subscription history** — kept until you delete your account (required for billing dispute resolution)
- **Push notification token** — kept until you uninstall the app or disable notifications
- **Crash logs** — purged after 90 days
- **Aggregated analytics** — retained indefinitely in non-identifying form

When you delete your account, all data associated with you is removed from our database within 30 days, except aggregated analytics that no longer identifies you.

## Your rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and all associated data — from Settings > Delete Account
- **Export** your data (data portability)
- **Restrict or object** to processing
- **Withdraw consent** where processing is based on consent (does not apply to data necessary for the service)

To exercise any of these rights, email **digest.app.support@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. In the EU you can find your authority at https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

### EU representative

We do not currently have a designated EU representative under GDPR Article 27. For data protection inquiries, contact us directly at **digest.app.support@gmail.com** — we commit to responding within 30 days.

## Children

Digest is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to Digest, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change. Significant updates will be announced in-app.

## Contact

For privacy requests, email **digest.app.support@gmail.com**.

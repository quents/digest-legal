# Digest — Support & Legal

Digest is an iOS app that reads the videos, articles, threads and PDFs you save and gives you a short AI summary.

## Support

Need help or have a question? Email **digest.app.support@gmail.com**. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

### Common questions

- **How do I save something?** In Safari, YouTube, X, Reddit, Photos or Files, tap the Share button and choose Digest. The summary appears in the app a few seconds later.
- **Digest isn't in my share sheet.** In the share sheet, scroll the app row to the end, tap More, and turn on Digest. You can also open Settings → How to save in the app.
- **How do I manage or cancel Digest Pro?** Subscriptions are handled by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Digest. Canceling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.
- **I'm not getting notifications.** Check Settings → Notifications → Digest, and make sure a Focus mode isn't silencing it.
- **How do I delete my account?** In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This removes your saves and summaries and revokes Sign in with Apple.
